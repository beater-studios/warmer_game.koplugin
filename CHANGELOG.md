## [0.1.0]

First version.

- Word of the day and free play in Portuguese, English and Spanish, with the interface in the same three languages.
- Each guess shows its position in a ranking of closeness in meaning, with a bar on a log scale.
- The game's own keyboard, always on screen; accents are optional and plurals count as their singular.
- A word sent again turns the list to its page and is marked as already sent.
- Hints halfway to the secret word, giving up with the 100 closest words revealed.
- Streak, wins and average guesses by language, and the daily result as a QR code without revealing any word.
- Three package sizes, with 100, 150 (recommended) and 300 dimensions.
